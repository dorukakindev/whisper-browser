# Historical reports and handoff records

The root `BROWSER_BUG_REPORT_*.md`, `PROGRAM_BUG_REPORT_*.md`, dated audit files,
and `docs/devir/` notes are retained as engineering history. They show what was
observed at a particular commit; they are **not** a current defect list or a
support promise.

Use these current sources first:

- `README.md` — product overview and normal setup.
- `SECURITY.md` — private vulnerability reporting and supported security flow.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md` — contribution and verification rules.
- `FEATURE_RECOMMENDATION_CATALOG_2026-09-20.md` — feature decisions and current
  implemented/conditional status.
- `docs/KABUL_MATRISI_2026-09-20.md` — release evidence and explicit manual gaps.
- `DEVIR-NOTU.md` — newest handoff entry, followed by immutable dated notes.

Do not treat a historical “open”, “fixed”, or line number as current without
checking the referenced commit and present source. Do not add credentials,
browser profiles, cookies, personal screenshots, copyrighted video, or full
subtitle bodies to these records.
