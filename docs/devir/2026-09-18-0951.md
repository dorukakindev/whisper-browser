2. **Durable CEA cue recovery.** New `src/browser-cea-checkpoint.js` keeps bounded, atomic, seven-day cue snapshots in the application's `userData/capture-checkpoints` folder, keyed by SHA-256 of the query-free manifest path, media identity, and CEA channels. It never persists signed URLs, tokens, or a completed-segment skip ledger. `src/main.js` saves the first caption-bearing progress and batches subsequent writes, restores captions when full capture is explicitly started after an app restart, and removes the snapshot on verified completion or explicit cancel/dismiss.
