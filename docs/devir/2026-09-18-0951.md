## Three requested changes

1. **Page-translation toolbar keyboard focus.** `src/browser-page-translate.js` no longer removes the `:focus-visible` outline. Hover retains its amber fill; keyboard focus has a distinct white 2px ring. `tests/browser-page-translate.test.js` checks the generated injected CSS contract.
2. **Durable CEA cue recovery.** New `src/browser-cea-checkpoint.js` keeps bounded, atomic, seven-day cue snapshots in the application's `userData/capture-checkpoints` folder, keyed by SHA-256 of the query-free manifest path, media identity, and CEA channels. It never persists signed URLs, tokens, or a completed-segment skip ledger. `src/main.js` saves the first caption-bearing progress and batches subsequent writes, restores captions when full capture is explicitly started after an app restart, and removes the snapshot on verified completion or explicit cancel/dismiss. The mux.js decoder state cannot be serialized: a restored job **replays the segment plan from the beginning** to verify continuity; this is caption recovery, not zero-network resumability. `tests/browser-cea-checkpoint.test.js` covers identity renewal, redaction, expiry, cleanup, and the decoder-safe replay contract.

PNGs remain in ignored `.uiprev/browser-menu-visual/`; they are not committed. Run with `node tests/run-electron-smokes.js browser-menu-visual`.
